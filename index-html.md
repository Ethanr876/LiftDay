## Scope
index.html, the whole app. Mode: Operate. Athlete at the rack, phone in one hand, between sets.

## Task
Enter maxes and bodyweight once; enter today's workout; see the next set's weight and plates per side, check sets off, rest between them. States: empty card, missing max, lighter than the bar, resting, rest over, card done, PR.
